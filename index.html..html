Layer lab

Choose two fragrances to see how they behave together, or choose one to see its best partners.

A starter library of 65 fragrances. Notes for the Hawas line, Asad, 9PM, Club de Nuit Untold, Yara and Amber Oud Gold were checked against retailer and Parfumo listings, which sometimes disagree. The rest were typed from memory. Strength ratings and price bands are approximate. "Same direction as" reflects what reviewers say, not a promise that two scents smell alike. Try on skin before you buy.
